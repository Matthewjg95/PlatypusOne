## Known limitations this battery documents

- A washer's outer diameter reads through the across-flats table as a
  conditional nominal (valid only if the part is a nut); nut_vs_washer
  stays unresolved until a side profile lands.
- Bolt nominals match the shank only; a head-dominated top-down outline
  will overstate the nominal (see ai.fastener_classifier.v2 notes).
- The width measurement (minimum support width, 1-deg sweep) is
  rotation-invariant; the earlier principal-axis extent overestimated
  rotated hexagons and is retired as of vision.scout_analyzer.v2.
